# ALRYA — Product Scope

## Problem

Hospitality businesses often operate acquisition, conversations, reservations and revenue across disconnected systems. ALRYA is designed to preserve the commercial context across that journey.

## Product direction

```text
Marketing → Conversation → Quote → Reservation → Revenue → Operations
```

## Main areas

- **Revenue:** connects commercial activity to reservation and revenue outcomes.
- **Omnichannel Chats:** preserves conversation history and context.
- **Assistant:** applies AI inside operational workflows.
- **Marketing / Studio:** connects campaign work, creative production and performance context.
- **Agents:** organizes specialized AI-assisted workflows.
- **Integrations:** isolates provider-specific adapters from the product core.
- **Billing / Admin:** supports account, organization, access and commercial administration.

## Principles

- Preserve context across the complete journey.
- Treat multi-tenancy as a domain concern.
- Keep operational truth in the appropriate source system.
- Apply AI to real workflows.
- Keep customer data and implementation secrets private.
